Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about installation, permissions, download access, licensing, and privacy.

What macOS version is required?

Workbench requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later and runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

How do I install Workbench?

After paying with PayPal, return to the QnSub confirmation page in the same browser and download the zip. Unzip it, move Workbench.app to Applications, then open it. If you bought through Gumroad before, use your Gumroad library for your download.

macOS blocked the app from opening. What should I do?

Make sure you opened the download from your confirmed QnSub purchase or your previous Gumroad purchase. If macOS still blocks launch, email support@qnsub.com with the exact message and your macOS version.

Does Workbench replace the built-in macOS file browser?

No. The built-in macOS file browser stays on your Mac. Workbench adds a focused workspace for heavier file tasks such as dual-pane browsing, media preview, notes, snippets, cleanup, and disk analysis.

Why does Workbench ask for Screen Recording or Microphone permission?

Screen Recording is optional and only needed for screenshot or recording tools. Microphone is optional and only needed for voice recording, voice notes, and narrated recordings. You can change both permissions in macOS System Settings.

Does Workbench upload my files?

No. Workbench works locally on your Mac. It does not include analytics, cloud sync, advertising SDKs, or background uploads.

Can I use one purchase on multiple Macs?

Yes. One Workbench purchase covers personal or family use within one household on up to 5 Macs. The household license is not for company-wide sharing, resale, or public redistribution.

How do I download again after paying?

Keep the QnSub confirmation link and reopen it in the same browser. The browser’s checkout cookie lasts up to 14 days. If it expired, you cleared your cookies, or you used a different browser, email support@qnsub.com with the transaction ID from your PayPal receipt for manual recovery. Never send your card number, password, or verification code. For a previous Gumroad purchase, check your Gumroad library.

Where can I read the privacy policy?

The full policy is available at /workbench/privacy.
